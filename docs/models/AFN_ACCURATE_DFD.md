## Why This Is The Accurate DFD

This DFD follows the actual codebase flow:

1. Client account and request data are processed.
2. Admin/superadmin reviews requests and dispatches service tickets.
3. Technician handles assigned jobs, checklist completion, proof uploads, and inventory usage.
4. Completed tickets continue into after-sales, maintenance, messages, and notifications.
5. Admin/superadmin monitors reports, analytics, dashboards, and activity logs.

## Data Stores Used

- D1 User Records
- D2 Service Request Records
- D3 Service Ticket Records
- D4 Checklist and Proof Records
- D5 Inventory Records
- D6 After-Sales and Maintenance Records
- D7 Message and Notification Records
- D8 Report, Analytics, and Activity Log Records
